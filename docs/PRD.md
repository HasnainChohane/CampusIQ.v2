# DepartmentHub — Product Requirements Summary

**Version**: 1.0 (Hackathon MVP)  
**Vision**: Centralized departmental workspace for academic, operational, and financial management with grounded AI assistance.

## Core Modules
1. **Academic**: Students, Faculty, Courses, Workload, Enrollments.
2. **Operations & Inventory**: Hardware, Lab equipment, Consumables, Conditions, Assignments.
3. **Requests & Approval Workflow**: Multi-role workflow (Staff/Faculty submit -> AI analysis & extraction -> Officer/Admin review -> Approve/Reject/Return).
4. **Finance**: Revenue, Expenses, Annual Budgets, Revenue Targets, Financial KPIs.
5. **AI Services**: Request classification & extraction, grounded assistant Q&A, automated insights, executive PDF report summaries.
6. **Reports**: PDF export of Academic, Financial, Inventory, and Performance records.
